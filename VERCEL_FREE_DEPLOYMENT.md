## 🎯 Vercel Deployment Steps:

### Step 1: Go to Vercel
1. Visit **vercel.com**
2. **Sign up with GitHub**
3. **No payment required!**

### Step 2: Deploy
1. Click **"New Project"**
2. **Import your GitHub repository**
3. **Framework Preset**: Other
4. **Build Command**: `echo "Static deployment"`
5. **Output Directory**: `client`
6. Click **"Deploy"**
